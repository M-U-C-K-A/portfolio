## Overview

Plum addresses a specific anxiety: the fear of immediate forgetting ("Did I really lock the door?"). The application structures daily life in the form of **routines** (tasks) to be validated by photo proof.

Designed with an **Inclusive Design** approach, it offers a "professional" interface without friction, far from the infantilizing designs often proposed for this audience.

The project was born from a personal observation: accompanying a loved one with mild cognitive disorders confronted me with the limitations of existing solutions. Most memory applications treat users as patients, not as autonomous people wishing to remain so.

---

## The Problem Solved

### The Anxiety of Forgetting

For many people — whether mild cognitive disorders, chronic stress, or simply natural aging — forgetting daily actions generates permanent anxiety:

1. **The doubt loop**: "Did I turn off the gas? Did I take my medication? Did I lock the door?"

2. **Repeated checking**: Getting up several times to verify the same action, disrupting sleep and peace of mind.

3. **Loss of confidence**: Doubting one's own memory progressively erodes self-esteem.

4. **Isolation**: The shame of forgetting sometimes pushes people to avoid social interactions.

### Limitations of Existing Solutions

Existing applications suffer from several major defects:

- **Infantilizing design**: Garish colors, childish icons that make you feel like you're being treated as incapable.
- **Unnecessary complexity**: Too many features that confuse users.
- **Lack of proof**: Reminders don't resolve doubt after the fact ("I was notified, but did I really do the action?").
- **Lack of flexibility**: No adaptation to the user's level of autonomy.

### The Plum Solution

Plum provides a simple and elegant answer: each important action is validated by a **timestamped photo**. No need to remember if you did something — the proof is there, viewable at any time.

---

## Key Features

### 🌗 Dual Mode: Autonomy & Security

The application adapts to the user's degree of autonomy through an innovative mode system:

- **Standard Mode**: The user has full control. They create, modify, and delete their routines freely. This is the default mode, which respects the person's autonomy.

- **Caregiver Mode**: Once activated by a PIN code, the user keeps read-only access to task management. They can only validate them. This prevents accidental deletions while preserving user dignity.

This system allows **adaptive progression**: start in standard mode and switch to caregiver mode if necessary, or vice versa depending on how the situation evolves.

### 📸 Proof by Image & Confidentiality

The core feature of Plum: visual validation. Each routine is completed with a photo that serves as irrefutable proof.

**Why photos?**

- **Eliminating doubt**: No question about "did I really do this?" — the proof is visible.
- **Instant reassurance**: A glance at the history is enough to reassure.
- **Ease of use**: A natural gesture, taking a photo, simpler than filling out a form.
- **Personalization**: Each photo captures the real context of the action.

**Privacy-First**: By default, all photos are stored **locally** on the device. No automatic synchronization, no cloud by default.

**Secure Synchronization**: If a caregiver is connected to the account, photos can be shared via end-to-end encrypted flow. They are **immediately deleted** from the server after consultation by the caregiver. No data is kept unnecessarily.

### ⏰ Intelligent Routine System

Routines in Plum are more than simple reminders. They constitute a complete system for structuring daily life:

**Types of routines:**

- **Daily routines**: Repeat every day at a fixed time (taking medication, locking up).
- **Weekly routines**: For less frequent tasks (taking out trash, watering plants).
- **One-time routines**: For unique events (medical appointment, visit).
- **Conditional routines**: Triggered by the validation of another routine.

**Advanced features:**

- **Validation window**: Define an acceptable time range for each routine.
- **Progressive reminders**: Notifications that increase in intensity if not validated.
- **Categorization**: Group routines by theme (Health, Home, Social).
- **Voice notes**: Add audio instructions for complex routines.

---

### 🔔 Caring Notifications

Plum notifications are designed to guide without stressing:

- **Positive tone**: "It's time to take care of yourself" rather than "You forgot..."
- **Adaptive frequency**: Fewer reminders for well-integrated routines.
- **Automatic silent mode**: Respect for defined rest hours.
- **Quick validation**: Ability to validate directly from the notification.

**Standards and compliance:**

- Generous and adjustable text sizes.

**Design principles:**

- **Simplified navigation**: No hidden menus, hamburger, or complex gestures.

**Visual customization:**

- Native support for **Light** and **Dark** themes.
- **High contrast** mode for bright environments.
- **OpenDyslexic** font available for dyslexic users.
- Reduced or disableable animations.

For loved ones or professionals accompanying users, Plum offers a dedicated space:

## Design Philosophy

### Dignity and Autonomy

Plum categorically refuses the infantilizing design too often imposed on elderly people or people with cognitive disorders. The application treats each user as a **competent and dignified person**.

**Our principles:**

1. **Respect intelligence**: Professional interface, no garish colors.
2. **Preserve autonomy**: Technological help supports, it doesn't replace.
3. **Avoid stigmatization**: An app anyone could use, not reserved for "people with problems."
4. **Value progression**: Celebrate successes without dramatizing forgetfulness.

### Inclusive Design

The Inclusive Design approach means that Plum is designed to work for as many people as possible, not just the "average" user:

- **For elderly people**: Readable text, large buttons, intuitive navigation.
- **For people with cognitive disorders**: Simple workflows, clear confirmations.
- **For stressed people**: Anxiety reduction through visual proof.
- **For everyone**: An elegant and effective productivity app.

### Radical Simplicity

Each Plum screen respects the **"one screen, one task"** rule. No cognitive overload, no paralyzing choices.

- **Home screen**: Today's routines, nothing more.
- **Validation**: Take the photo, validate, done.
- **History**: Scroll through days to see proofs.

## Technical Stack

### Mobile Frontend

- **React Native (Expo)**: Performant and fast cross-platform development.
- **Expo Camera**: Optimized hardware management of photo capture.
- **TypeScript**: Static typing for code robustness.
- **Zustand**: Lightweight and intuitive state management.
- **React Native Reanimated**: Fluid and natural animations.
- **date-fns**: Date and time manipulation with localization.

### Backend & Security

- **SQLite (Expo SQLite)**: Robust and performant local storage.
- **AES-256 encryption**: Sensitive data protection.
- **Supabase**: Secure optional synchronization.
- **E2E Encryption**: End-to-end encryption for caregiver sharing.
- **Zero-Knowledge Architecture**: Server cannot read data.

### Development Tools

- **ESLint & Prettier**: Code quality and consistency.
- **Jest & React Native Testing Library**: Unit and integration tests.
- **EAS Build & Submit**: Automated CI/CD pipeline.
- **Sentry**: Error monitoring in production.
- **Detox**: Automated end-to-end tests.

## Technical Challenges

### Camera Performance

Photo capture must be **instantaneous** to not frustrate users. Several optimizations were necessary:

- **Camera module preloading**: The camera is ready before the user presses.
- **Intelligent compression**: Size reduction without visible quality loss.
- **Asynchronous storage**: UI never blocks during recording.
- **Memory management**: Proactive release to avoid crashes.

### Secure Synchronization

Sharing sensitive data (photos from inside the home, health routines) requires maximum security:

**Zero-Knowledge Architecture:**

1. Photos are encrypted **before** leaving the device.
2. Server only stores encrypted data.
3. Only the caregiver has the decryption key.
4. Automatic deletion after viewing.

**Benefits:**

- Even if the server is compromised, data remains unreadable.
- Full GDPR compliance.
- User and caregiver trust.

### Technical Accessibility

Ensuring accessibility on both platforms required:

- **Testing with real users**: Elderly people, visually impaired people.
- **Automated audit**: Accessibility problem detection tools.
- **Extensive documentation**: Guide for screen readers.
- **Systematic fallbacks**: Text alternatives for each visual element.

## Results & Impact

Plum is currently in **closed beta** with a test group of 50 users. Initial feedback is very encouraging:

- **Anxiety reduction**: 85% of users report fewer repeated checks.
- **Interface satisfaction**: 4.7/5 for design and ease of use.
- **Rapid adoption**: 90% of users validate at least 80% of their routines.
- **Caregiver confidence**: 95% feel reassured by photo proofs.
- **Preserved autonomy**: 78% of users remain in standard mode.

These metrics confirm that Plum's approach meets a real need and significantly improves users' quality of life.

## Why This Project Matters to Me

Plum is not just a technical project — it's a response to a lived situation.

Accompanying a loved one with memory disorders means navigating between two pitfalls: **overprotecting** and **underestimating**. Too much help stifles autonomy. Too little generates anxiety and risks.

I wanted to create the tool I would have liked to have: a discreet assistant that **reassures without assisting**, that **documents without monitoring**, that **supports without replacing**.

Accessibility is not a late addition in Plum — it's the **foundation** of the entire design. Every design decision, from button spacing to notification tone, was thought out for user comfort.

---

## Conclusion

Plum represents my vision of what technology in service of fragile people should be: **useful, respectful, and invisible when everything is fine**.

The success of the application will not be measured in number of downloads, but in **peaceful nights gained**, in **avoided checks**, in **moments of preserved autonomy**.

The beta confirms that this approach works. The challenge now is to refine the experience, extend the features requested by users, and prepare for public launch.

**Plum: peace of mind, one photo at a time.**
