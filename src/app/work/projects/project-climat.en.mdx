## Overview

Project Climate is an editorial project born from the desire to **make climate issues understandable**, without sensationalism or information overload.

Rather than flooding users with numbers or anxiety-inducing alerts, the goal was to **create a calm space**, designed for long reading, reflection, and nuance.

The approach focused on three axes:

- **Readability first**
- **Simple and non-aggressive navigation**
- **Pedagogical tone, not militant**

---

## Key Features

- **Structured and narrative articles**
- **Typography optimized for long reading**
- **Clear information hierarchy**
- **Readable and accessible dark mode**
- **Tag system for exploring topics**

---

## Design Approach

The interface is deliberately **silent**:
no unnecessary animations, no graphic overload.

Principles used:

- Generous spacing
- Stable grid
- Neutral colors
- Minimal accent
- Focus on **visual breathing**

The goal is to **remain in service of reflection**, not to distract.

---

## Technologies Used

- Next.js + React
- TypeScript
- TailwindCSS (Atomic Design approach)
- Markdown/MDX for article editing

---

## Outcome

Project Climate now serves as a solid base for:

- publishing articles,
- organizing knowledge,
- structuring analyses and syntheses.

It is designed as a **learning tool** as much as a media platform.

This project demonstrates the importance of **designing slow interfaces**, meant for reading, attention, and nuance.
